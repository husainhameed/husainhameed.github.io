Human Deception

Threat: Social Engineering
  • Hackers hack people. It is easier to manipulate your emotions than to write complex code to break into a server.
  • Attackers weaponize trust, fear, and urgency. "You won a prize!" or "Your account will be deleted in 24 hours!"
  • Beware of fake Instagram DMs and WhatsApp messages pretending to be official services or stranded friends.

Brute Force

Threat: Weak Credentials
  • Password Reuse is Fatal: If you use the same password for Netflix and your Bank, a breach on one site compromises your entire life.
  • Hackers use automated "Credential Stuffing" tools to test thousands of leaked passwords per second.
  • Stop trying to remember complex passwords. Use a Password Manager and prioritize length (passphrases) over complexity.

Invisible Interception

Threat: Public Wi-Fi
  • That "Free Airport Wi-Fi" might actually be a hacker's laptop sitting two rows behind you.
  • Man-in-the-Middle (MitM) Attacks: Attackers can silently intercept and read unencrypted data passing between your phone and the router.
  • Never log into BenefitPay, your primary email, or any sensitive account on a public network without a VPN.

The Eternal Record

Threat: Digital Footprint
  • Every post builds a permanent profile. Universities and employers are actively looking at your history.
  • Delete ≠ Erased. Screenshots, cloud backups, and internet archives ensure that nothing is ever truly gone.
  • Your digital footprint is the exact data hackers use to figure out how to scam you personally.

Synthetic Reality

Threat: AI Manipulation
  • Seeing is no longer believing. AI can generate hyper-realistic videos and clone voices from short clips.
  • AI allows attackers to scale their operations, writing flawless phishing emails in seconds.
  • Always independently verify urgent requests for money or data.
